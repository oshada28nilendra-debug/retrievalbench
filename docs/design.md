# RetrievalBench MVP
Purpose: help AI engineers compare passage retrieval before introducing answer generation. Approved inputs are small text/Markdown documents and questions annotated with relevant passage IDs.
A local Python/Flask app and CLI share validation, passage construction, TF-IDF and pinned MiniLM ONNX inference, deterministic rankings, and metric calculation. No paid APIs or answer generation.
Passages are nonempty blank-line-separated paragraphs, preserving Markdown as plain text. IDs are filename#p001 etc.; filenames and contents contribute to a corpus SHA. Users download a manifest before annotating. Relevance labels require existing unique passage IDs, at least one relevant passage per query. JSON can carry corpus_sha256 to reject stale annotations; missing fingerprints produce a warning.
Bounds: 100 documents, 500 passages, 100 questions, 5 MiB total documents, 1 MiB questions, 20k characters per paragraph. No archives, paths, crawling or executable document content. Model truncation at 256 tokens is recorded.
Compare TF-IDF fitted on passages only (not questions) and fixed all-MiniLM-L6-v2 embeddings. Retrieve positive cosine scores, tie-break lexical passage IDs, compute macro Recall@k and MRR@k. Per-query output includes ranked text and missed relevant IDs; timings separate model load, index, first-query warmup and sequential query encode/search latencies.
Synthetic evaluation is explicitly authored, not evidence of production quality; no labelled fitting/tuning. Tests cover known ranks/metrics, no-match queries, validation failures, stale fingerprints, transport, actual pretrained model and browser report export.
